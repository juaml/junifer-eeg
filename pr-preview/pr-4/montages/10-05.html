10-20 system, and its 10-10 and 10-05 extensions¶

System: 10-20 system, and its 10-10 and 10-05 extensions (e.g., ANT Neuro waveguard, Brain Products actiCAP, EasyCap, BioSemi ActiveTwo up to 64 channels, Compumedics Neuroscan Quik-Cap, g.tec g.GAMMAcap, Nexstim eXimia, and the clinical 10-20 systems, as Natus, Nihon Kohden or Micromed).

ROIs: scalp, nonscalp, fz, cz, pz.

Montages (the MNE montage with the positions):

  • colin27_1005: 10-05 positions, on the Colin27 template head

  • colin27_1020: 10-20 and 10-10 positions, on the Colin27 template head

  • fsaverage_1005: 10-05 positions, on the fsaverage head

  • fsaverage_1010: 10-10 positions, on the fsaverage head

  • fsaverage_1020: 10-20 positions, on the fsaverage head

  • spherical_1010: 10-10 positions, on a spherical head

  • spherical_1020: 10-20 positions, on a spherical head

  • biosemi16: BioSemi ActiveTwo, 16 channels

  • biosemi32: BioSemi ActiveTwo, 32 channels

  • biosemi64: BioSemi ActiveTwo, 64 channels

  • easycap-M1: EasyCap M1 (e.g., Brain Products actiCAP), 10-10 positions

  • brainproducts-RNP-BA-128: Brain Products R-Net, 128 channels

The fz, cz and pz ROIs are the 10-05 positions in the region (the convex hull, on the topomap) of the union of the ROIs of the ANT 32, BrainVision 32, eximia 60 and g.tec 12 caps (the indices of the ANT 63 and 124 ROIs do not match their channels). The picks keep the channels in the data.

The channels of each ROI (highlighted), on the positions of colin27_1005. The picks keep the channels of the ROI that are in the data.
