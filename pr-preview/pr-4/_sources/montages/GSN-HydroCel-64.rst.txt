EGI HydroCel Geodesic Sensor Net, 64 channels
=============================================

System: EGI HydroCel Geodesic Sensor Net, 64 channels.

ROIs: ``scalp``, ``nonscalp``, ``Fz``, ``Cz``, ``Pz``, ``cnv``, ``mmn``, ``p3a``, ``p3b``.

Montages (the MNE montage with the positions):

* ``GSN-HydroCel-64_1.0``: EGI HydroCel Geodesic Sensor Net, 64 channels
* ``GSN-HydroCel-65_1.0``: EGI HydroCel Geodesic Sensor Net, 64 channels and the vertex reference (Cz)

The channels of each ROI (highlighted), on the positions of ``GSN-HydroCel-64_1.0``. The ``picks`` keep the channels of the ROI that are in the data.
