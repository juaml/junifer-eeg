Welcome to junifer-eeg's documentation!
=======================================

``junifer-eeg`` is a `junifer`_ extension for EEG data. It reimplements the
feature extraction of `NICE`_ (the EEG markers of consciousness) as junifer
pipeline components, configured entirely with `YAML`_: reading the
recordings, preprocessing them with `MNE-Python`_ and computing the markers.

It is developed and maintained at the Applied Machine Learning (`AML`_) group
at Forschungszentrum Juelich, Germany.
